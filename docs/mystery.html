The Bellweather House murder

Eight agents are spending a night in a house where the owner has been killed. Each has private information and goals. They can investigate, form chats, invite each other, share evidence, or lie.

The setting borrows from The Mysterious Affair at Styles. The characters, clues, and solution are new. We fixed the facts before play; the agents choose what to do with them.

We want to follow how people arrive at a shared story: where a claim starts, who changes it, and what gets left out when it moves between private and public conversations.

How this run works

Each character has a separate, persistent CAOS conversation using Claude Opus 4.8. In each round they choose actions from their own view of the game. A controller applies the actions and delivers the resulting messages in the next round. Joining a chat does not reveal its earlier messages.

The model adapter removes the coding tools; characters act through the game's action list. It also enforces a $50 spending ceiling and a 9 a.m. Pacific cutoff on September 20. The game has 18 rounds, ending with private accusations and an estate vote.

The chats are game records linked to CAOS conversations. They are not native CAOS subagent branches. The saved events are checked against the original model replies, and the controller's rules are replayed to check membership and delivery.

This is fictional roleplay with assigned secrets and goals. We have not picked an incident in advance or scripted anyone's dialogue.
