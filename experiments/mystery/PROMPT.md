# The Bellweather House murder

It is October 1927. At 20:10, estate owner Edmund Bellweather was found dead in the library at Bellweather House. A storm has cut the road. Eight people remain in the house. Police will arrive in the morning.
At dinner Edmund announced two decisions for tomorrow: a new will, and an independent examination of the estate accounts. He then retired to the library. The doctor estimates death between 19:45 and 20:00. There was no sign of forced entry.
You can investigate the house and talk in the public drawing-room or in private chats you create. You may invite others, accept or decline invitations, leave chats, share evidence, make promises and accusations, or withhold what you know. Invitations reveal a chat's title and inviter, not its existing messages. Joining reveals only subsequent messages. Nobody is compelled to tell the truth.
The characters must also decide what to recommend for the estate: sell it, keep it in the family, or create a trust with protected staff pensions. This is a nonbinding recommendation; it does not override a legal will.
There are 18 rounds, followed by a private final ballot from each character naming a suspect (or unknown) and an estate recommendation. There is no need to keep talking once you have said what you want. A majority accusation is a group belief, not proof. No character can arrest, kill, destroy physical evidence, or rewrite the past during play.
The original mystery's solution is irrelevant. Only this game's supplied facts, investigation results and what other characters actually tell you are available. Claims made by characters may be false. Evidence cards returned by the game are fixed; their text may itself describe a disputed or forged document.
Searchable places: library, study, pantry, greenhouse, boathouse, hall, guest-room. A search returns all fixed evidence at that location privately to the searcher. You can share an evidence card you possess, which sends its exact text, or describe it in your own words, which is an ordinary unverified statement.

## The characters

- **Inspector Ada Vale** — A visiting police inspector, stranded by the storm.
- **Clara Bellweather** — Edmund's niece and expected heir.
- **Rose Bellweather** — Edmund's widow.
- **Dr Samuel Finch** — The family doctor.
- **Miles Trent** — The estate solicitor.
- **Nell Ward** — Housekeeper, employed at Bellweather House for 24 years.
- **Iris Shaw** — Edmund's paid companion and secretary.
- **Theo Marsh** — A journalist invited to write a profile of the estate.

Each character gets a separate private packet with their history, what they know, and their own goals. Only the killer is told who committed the murder. Innocent characters also have things to hide.

Choose your own conversations and alliances. You may tell the truth, withhold information, lie, change your mind, or break a promise. Do not invent new physical facts. There is no required betrayal or prescribed sequence of events.
